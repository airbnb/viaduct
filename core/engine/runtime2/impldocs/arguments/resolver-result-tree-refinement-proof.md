# Argument for [resolver-result-tree-refinement-proof]

`tla/ResultTree.tla` flattens a finite, well-founded primary OER into object-occurrence, cell-occurrence, and resolver-observation atoms. List positions are part of occurrence identity, and `ReachableObjects` is the intersection-defined least set containing the Query root and closed under children of present cells. `tla/ResultTreeProof.tla` proves reachability closure and finiteness, then proves that the local fragment, resolver-demand, resolver-observation, and typename judgments at every reachable object are equivalent to the corresponding whole-tree conjuncts of the modeled `CorrectResolution`. Nonempty resolver Query fragments and the additional occurrence-specific Query OERs required by Kotlin `correctResolution` are outside this TLA+ refinement claim.

`tla/TreeConstruction.tla` indexes least exact-key demand closure by object occurrence. `tla/TreeConstructionProof.tla` proves that completed folds contain operation demand and every activated resolver's exact direct demand, that empty Resolver01 fragments leave demand unchanged, and that both Resolver01 and Resolver02 construction postconditions establish whole-tree fragment and resolver-demand conformance. This is the non-tautological replacement for the former `Resolver02RecursiveLifting` statement.

`tla/Projection.tla` gives `snipToDemand` a finite observation semantics: passive observations whose exact guarded demand coverage intersects the supplied demand are copied from one selection-independent raw output, while registered resolver observations are boundaries. TLAPS proves projection completeness, soundness, monotonicity, boundary stopping, and agreement on overlap. `tla/ValueConstruction.tla` aligns each result-tree resolver observation with that projection and the independently interpreted raw resolver output, so TLAPS derives resolver conformance; the resolver-output contract and root resolver supply typename correctness. `tla/Resolver03ApplicationProof.tla` derives the necessary projection coverage from Resolver03 demand construction and composes the same extensional judgment with prefix materialization.

The proof remains conditional on a faithful extraction of Kotlin schema, selection, value, list-position, and resolver-output structure into the finite atoms and relations. `tla/OccurrenceFolds.tla` now proves simultaneous completion of every occurrence-indexed fold, but `OutputAlignment` still must identify its terminal built keys with the returned OER's `PresentKeys`. Structural Kotlin `materializeResult`, `resolvePassiveValues`, `observedDemand`, and `engineResultConformsToResolverValue` also have not yet been proved to induce exactly the modeled observation alignment. TLC models provide finite counterexample-finding evidence for the encodings but do not discharge those refinement obligations.
